# NUQA — Updated innovation presentation

14 slides in English, retaining the original NUQA branding and team attribution.

- [View the PDF](NUQA_Updated.pdf)
- [Editable PowerPoint](NUQA_Updated.pptx)

The presentation covers the problem and proposed solution, current citizen/worker/officer interface screenshots, vehicle littering and fictional plate evidence, lighting-pole camera concepts for public parks, the response workflow and a controlled pilot plan.

The road and park scenes are AI-generated concept illustrations. Application screenshots show the implemented browser-based prototype. Real camera analysis, plate recognition, authority integration and enforcement are proposed capabilities, not deployed functions. All simulated vehicles, incidents and plates are fictional.
